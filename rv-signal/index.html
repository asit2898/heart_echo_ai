THE CLINICAL CONTEXT

Follow-up after treatment

The paper describes a patient with heritable PAH before and six months after starting sotatercept. The care team used many clinical findings, not ultrasound alone.

AT THE REVIEW DESK

How this informs care

  1. Read the standard echo measurements and inspect image quality.
  2. Compare symptoms, walking test, and blood results with the previous visit.
  3. Discuss the complete response and treatment plan with the PAH team.

An AI trace could help clinicians check and contextualize right-heart motion. It cannot recommend a drug or override the report.
